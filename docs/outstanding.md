## Replace the logo (squatting figure)

**Why:** the stick figure squatting inside the ring reads like a restroom or accessibility sign, not a fitness app (feedback, 2026-10-04).

**Brief:** something more creative; it doesn't have to be a person exercising. It should:
- read at 32 px (browser tab) and at 1280×720 (Fire TV tile);
- use the Arena palette: volt `#C8FF3D` on ground `#0A0B10`;
- keep a link to the product: the tick ring, counting, "spotting" someone, phone + TV.

**Directions to explore:**
1. **The ring alone as the mark:** the progress ring of ticks with a bold "S" or a single lit tick, no figure.
2. **A spotter's eye:** an eye or lens shape built from the ring, since a spotter watches your form.
3. **A rep counter:** a big condensed number inside the ring, like a scoreboard.
4. **Phone-to-TV link:** two rounded rectangles (phone, TV) joined by an arc of ticks.

**Concepts (2026-10-04):** `brand/concepts/sheet.png`: A. Ring + S, B. Spotter's eye, C. Scoreboard, D. Phone to TV, E. The spot. Round 2, `brand/concepts/sheet-2.png`: F. Kettlebell S, G. Bolt S, H. Level up, I. Spot the mascot, J. Weight plate, K. Pulse check. Sources: `brand/concepts/make-concepts*.py`.

**Where it changes:** everything in `brand/README.md` → "Where it's used": the Fire TV launcher icon, the TV wordmark and coach card, the phone page header, favicon and home-screen icon, the README, and the store images.

**Store:** Spotter was submitted to the Amazon Appstore on 2026-10-04 with the current logo. A new logo means an app update: a new build with `build-number` raised to 2 in `tv-app/manifest.toml`, plus new icon, background and featured images in the console.

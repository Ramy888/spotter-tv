SPOTTER

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Spotter is a fitness app for Fire TV. Your phone's camera tracks your movement, and your TV counts your reps and coaches you. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Camera video never leaves your phone

Pose tracking runs on your phone, in the Spotter web page. No video or photo is sent or stored anywhere. Only 33 body points (positions like "left knee", as numbers) are sent, about 15 times a second, from your phone to your TV through our relay.

What we handle, and for how long

DataWhyWhere, and how long
Body points (numbers, no images)Counting reps on your TVPassed through our relay on Amazon Web Services in real time. Not stored.

A player's name (optional)So the coach can say it ("Let's go, Ramy!")Sent to Amazon Polly to make 5 short voice clips. The clips are kept on our server for 60 days, then removed automatically. Skip naming a player to avoid this.
Workout numbers (reps, pace, depth), never your nameOptional AI rewrite of the coach's summarySent to an AI model (Cloudflare Workers AI). Not stored by us.
Server logsKeeping the service runningAmazon Web Services, deleted after 7 days. No video, no names.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in, email or phone number.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and no cookies.
  • We don't sell or share personal data.

Children

Spotter is not directed at children under 13. Workouts with children should be supervised by an adult.

Health

Spotter gives general exercise feedback, not medical advice. Stop if you feel pain, and ask a doctor before starting a new exercise program.
